# Government Radar

Use the `govradar` tools for questions about recent Canadian government announcements: federal, Prime Minister's Office, and the ten provinces.

- `latest_releases`: newest releases, optional `jurisdiction` and `topic` filters.
- `search_releases`: keyword search (every word must match).
- `list_jurisdictions_and_topics`: valid filter ids.

Coverage is the last 7 days, up to 5 results per request. Cite the official release link for each item.
